# Try Plans on your own iPhone and laptop (testnet, about 10 minutes)

Everything here is on Monad **testnet** with free test dollars. Nothing costs real money.
Use one iPhone (Safari) and one laptop (Chrome or Safari). You play two friends.

## 1. Laptop: make an account and a plan (3 min)

1. Open **https://plans.0xo.in/app** in Chrome or Safari.
2. **Create account** → the browser looks for a Plans passkey first. None yet, so pick **I'm new to Plans**.
3. Save the passkey with Touch ID (or your Mac password). Type a name, pick a country. You're on Home.
4. **Add money → Get test dollars → Get $25 test dollars.** The balance turns into $25 within a couple of seconds.
5. **New plan** → name it, pick dates that include today, keep the **Balanced** rules → **Create plan**.
6. In the plan, **Add money** → $10 from your account.
7. **Invite** → **Copy link**. Send the link to your iPhone (AirDrop, Messages or Notes).

## 2. iPhone: join as a second person (3 min)

1. Open the invite link in **Safari**. It opens the plan's invite in the web app.
2. **Join with passkey** → Face ID; this creates the second account with an iPhone passkey and joins the plan.
3. **Add money** → **Get test dollars** first if the balance is $0, then put $10 in the plan.
4. Optional: **Share → Add to Home Screen**, open Plans from the Home Screen, then **You → Notifications → Turn on**. Notifications only work from the Home Screen on iPhone.

## 3. Spend, approve, settle (3 min)

1. **Laptop: Pay** $12 for "Lunch", category Food. It goes through at once (under $25). The iPhone's plan shows it within a second.
2. **iPhone: Pay** $40 for "Boat trip". It asks for one friend's OK.
3. **Laptop:** the request appears under **Needs you** → **Approve** with Touch ID. It runs.
4. **Laptop:** plan menu → **End plan** (or wait for the end date) → **Review & settle up** → **Looks right**.
5. **iPhone:** **Looks right**.
6. **Either device: Settle up.** One transaction pays everyone back. The receipt shows both currencies, the reference exchange rate and its source, and a **Proof** link to the transaction.

## 4. Link the laptop browser to the iPhone account (optional, 2 min)

1. Laptop, in a **private window**: https://plans.0xo.in/app → **Create account** → **Link this browser to your account**. A code, a QR and three pictures appear.
2. iPhone (signed in): **You → Devices with your passkey → Add a browser** → scan the QR or type the code.
3. Check the three pictures match, then confirm with Face ID. The laptop window is now the iPhone account.

## What to tell me

- Anything that looked wrong, slow, or confusing (a screenshot helps).
- Roughly how many seconds from **Create account** to seeing $25.
- Whether the receipt's rate line made sense.
