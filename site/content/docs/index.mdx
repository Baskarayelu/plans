Plans is a shared pot for trips, festivals, house shares and anything friends plan together, including friends who live in different countries. It is a native Android app on Monad.

- **Join with one fingerprint.** An invite link opens the app and a passkey creates the account. No seed phrase, no password, no gas token.
- **One pot in digital dollars.** Members add AUSD from wherever they live and see the same balance in their own currency.
- **Rules the group sets, enforced by the pot contract.** Instant spends below a limit, approvals above it, budgets per category, caps per person, a pause any member can press, and timelocked rule changes.
- **Settle up in one tap.** One transaction pays everyone what they are owed and collects from anyone who owes, across countries.

## Why it exists

A group plan usually runs on one person's card and a spreadsheet. One friend pays the villa deposit, another buys the festival tickets, and weeks later the group is still working out who owes whom, in three currencies. A shared bank account needs everyone to bank in the same country. A crypto wallet needs everyone to manage seed phrases and gas. Neither works for a group chat of friends.

## In one paragraph, for builders

Each plan is its own immutable `Pot` contract, deployed as an ERC-1167 clone by `PlansFactory`. Members never send transactions: they sign EIP-712 messages (and AUSD ERC-3009 authorisations for money in), which anyone can submit; our relayer does so and pays the gas. Accounts are passkeys through Mera, with a second PRF namespace (`plans.keys.v1`) for end-to-end encryption of receipts and names. An Envio HyperIndex indexer turns the events into balances, the who-owes-whom graph and public traction stats. See [Architecture](/docs/architecture).
