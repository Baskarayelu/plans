## Mainnet and testnet

There are two Android builds on the [download page](/download):

- **Plans** (`plans.apk`): Monad mainnet, real AUSD. This is the product being judged.
- **Plans Test** (`plans-test.apk`): Monad testnet, with free test dollars from the relayer's faucet, for unlimited experimenting. It installs alongside the real app. <Pending />

## What you need

- An Android phone, Android 9 or newer (10 or newer recommended). It works the same in the US and the UK; no SIM, VPN or local account needed.
- Signed in to a Google account, with a screen lock. Choose **Google Password Manager** if asked where to save the passkey.
- About 10 minutes.

## The judge path

<Steps>
<Step>
### Install

<Pending /> Open [plans.0xo.in/download](/download) on the phone and install the APK (its SHA-256 is on the page). Allow your browser to install unknown apps; if Play Protect warns, tap More details › Install anyway.
</Step>
<Step>
### Create your account

<Pending /> Tap Create account and confirm with your screen lock. Enter a name and pick a country. That one prompt is the whole sign-up.
</Step>
<Step>
### Get demo dollars

<Pending /> Open one of the claim links from the private judge field. Each holds $0.50 on mainnet and can be claimed once. Your balance shows $0.50 within about a second.
</Step>
<Step>
### Join the judges' plan

<Pending /> Open the judges' plan invite (private judge field) and tap Join. Spends up to $0.25 go through instantly; larger spends need one other member's approval.
</Step>
<Step>
### Core flow

<Pending /> 1. Add $0.25 with **Add money**. The pot balance updates.
2. Pay $0.10 from the pot to Asha (Bengaluru), Food & drink, split with everyone. It goes through instantly.
3. Try a $0.40 spend. The app shows "Needs 1 more approval". Ben (London) approves within about 10 seconds and it goes through.
4. Open **Send**, pick Asha and send $0.05. The receipt shows dollars and rupees, with the reference rate.
5. On Home tap **Try a settle-up**. It creates a short plan with the three demo members, adds a few spends, and lets you press **Settle up**. One transaction pays everyone. Tap **Proof** on any receipt to see the transaction on a Monad explorer.
</Step>
<Step>
### Fresh-device test

<Pending /> Android Settings › Apps › Plans › Storage › Clear storage. Open Plans, tap **I already use Plans**, confirm with your screen lock. Your account, plans, balances and encrypted receipts are rebuilt from your passkey alone, and the three-emoji key fingerprint under Profile matches the one before.
</Step>
</Steps>

## The demo accounts

**Ben (London), Asha (Bengaluru) and Maya (New York)** are demo members run by our relayer, so that one judge with one phone can see approvals and a multi-country settlement. The app labels them **Demo**. Every demo action is signed with that member's own key and goes through the same relay path and contract checks as yours.

- They approve pending spends up to $1 after a random 3–8 s, and confirm "Looks right" once the plan has ended or a human has.
- They are listed in [`indexer/internal-accounts.json`](https://github.com/Baskarayelu/plans/blob/main/indexer/internal-accounts.json) and **never counted** in our numbers, nor is any plan they join. See the [stats page](/stats).

## If something fails

- **"Passkey not supported":** make Google Password Manager your passkey provider (Android Settings › Passwords and accounts), then retry.
- **A link opens in the browser:** tap **Open in Plans** on the page, or copy the link and paste it into Plans › Join.
- **No test login exists:** Plans accounts are passkeys you create on your own phone.
